Infrastructure migration case study

Cutting infrastructure cost by ~20x while keeping production stable.

At Rewards Bunny, I moved an expensive managed-cloud setup toward Hetzner, CapRover, and a local database architecture — reducing infrastructure cost by approximately 20x while keeping backups, DNS/SSL, Docker deployments, database integrity, and continuity in view.

The problem

The cloud setup was becoming disproportionate for the company’s stage. The goal was not just “move servers”; it was to preserve a live product while changing the cost base.

Cost pressure

Cloud spend had to match stage.

A peak monthly charge of US$1,581.83 was too heavy for a lean rewards platform.

Production risk

Reliability still mattered.

Cashback, attribution, merchant data, and user trust meant migration mistakes could not be treated casually.

Founder tradeoff

Business and technical judgment met.

The decision required cost discipline, implementation fluency, and risk management rather than a purely technical preference.
